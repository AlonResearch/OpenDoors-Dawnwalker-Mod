# Open Doors — The Blood of Dawnwalker Mod

> **Freedom to enter. Freedom to leave.**

**Open Doors** is a quality-of-life and immersion mod for *The Blood of Dawnwalker*. It removes artificial door lock-ins, self-shutting gates, and invisible boundary walls that trap the player upon crossing the threshold of an area, room, or fortress.

---

## Why Open Doors?

In *The Blood of Dawnwalker*, crossing an open doorway into an interior or fortified area often triggers an abrupt, automatic door closure right behind you. Even when you were the one who pushed the heavy door open, and even when no guard or enemy is standing behind you to bolt it shut, the game seals the door and places an invisible barrier, denying you the ability to retreat.

This breaks immersion and restricts tactical freedom:
- **No Tactical Retreat**: If you peek into a room and realize you are underprepared or overwhelmed, you should be able to back out, regroup, and plan a better approach.
- **No Phantom Mechanisms**: Doors shouldn't slam shut by magic simply because your character stepped two paces past the doorframe. If an entrance was open, it stays open unless an actual in-world character physically locks or barricades it.
- **Natural Exploration**: Scout ahead, draw enemies out into open terrain, or use doorways as natural choke points without fear of getting locked in a killbox against your will.

---

## Mod Vision & Scope

- **Unrestricted Thresholds**: Keep doors and gates in their natural, open state after you interact with them.
- **Removal of Artificial Lock Walls**: Disable invisible collision volumes and one-way barrier volumes triggered on doorway entry.
- **Tactical Freedom**: Enable hit-and-run tactics, peeking, and scouting without arbitrary arena lockouts.
- **Immersion First**: Story-critical locked doors (e.g. key-required gates, structural cave-ins, or narrative cutscenes) remain intact where an actual physical barrier or narrative event justifies it.

---

## Planned Features

- [ ] **Persistent Open Doors**: Doors remain in their opened state after entering buildings, watchtowers, and fortresses.
- [ ] **Barrier Dissolution**: Disable entrance barrier collision boxes associated with non-boss interior encounter zones.
- [ ] **Flexible Encounter Boundaries**: Allow players to back out of encounters and reset or reposition naturally.
- [ ] **Configurable Behavior**: (Future) Optional toggles to choose between keeping doors open everywhere or allowing specific challenge modes.
